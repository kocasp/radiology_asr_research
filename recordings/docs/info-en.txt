The collected files come from various sources and are raw recordings.
They were not subjected to normalization - its choice depends on the user and the task at hand.

catalog human - contains recordings of untransformed human voice.
	anoni - are recordings of speakers subjected to the process of anonymization (SALT)
	synth - are recordings obtained with the help of a speech synthesizer - speachgen

Naming in the files:
MMNNN-AMVXXX-SSS-REC1-NN-NNN-TTTTTT-RRRMMDDTHHMMSS , where:

MM{ddd} - place of recording:
	PG{ddd} - HQ recording studios
	GU{ddd} - rooms on gumed MQ-LQ
	UNKNOV - online system, can be from LQ to HQ
	LQ{xxxxx} - does not exist at the moment, but it is synthesized and was ultimately intended as a model 
	
AMV{ddd} - technically a speaker ID, in the old approach synthesis also had its AMV, 
	AMV{ddd} - uploaded human voices (doctors, voiceovers)
	ANI{ddd} - anonymized human voices (transferring their color, gender)
	
FOR - here will always be for as forms
REC1 - id of the device they were recorded on (technically a microphone, e.g. TLM3 - neumann tlm3 etc., BEB5 - behringer b-5
 
NTSX -  RODE NT-SF1 (ambisonic, X=1:4)	
NTG2 - RODE NTG-2 (shotgun)	
SK24 - SENNHEISER ME 4-N (krawatowy)	
SH5 - ZOOM SSH-6 (stereo shotgun)	
ZM1X - Zylia Zm1 
ZOF2 - ZOOM F2		
BEB5 - Behringer B-5	
S416 - Sennheiser mkv416	
SU58 - SHURE SM58
TLM3 - Neumann TLM 103
TLM2 - Neumann TLM 102

{dd}-{ddd} - category codes
categoryName, 

02-001 - thyroid radiology
02-003 - head-neck radiology
03-000 - Oncology
05-000 - Cardiology 
06-000 - Surgical procedure course 
07-000 - Course of resuscitation 
08-000 - Recommendations (formally none)
09-000 - Referral 

TTTTTTT - six-digit text id - note, older uploads of texts with errors result in texts b similar, but with different id (sometimes white space, sometimes inflection, etc).
